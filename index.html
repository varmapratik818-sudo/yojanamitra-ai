Folder 01 · Food & Public Distribution

Ration Card Services

Maharashtra ration card aur Central Government food distribution services ki guidance ek jagah.

  • New ration card application guidance
  • Family member add / delete
  • Name, address and details correction
  • e-Ration Card and application status
  • Ration entitlement, FPS shop and grievance

1. New Ration Card

Check eligibility, household category, residence requirements and the applicable application process on the state portal.

  • Identity and address proof, as specified by the department
  • Family member details and required declarations
  • Existing ration card details, if applicable
  • Additional documents may be requested by the authority

2. Add or Remove a Family Member

Choose the appropriate member addition or deletion service. Keep the existing ration card and supporting family documents available. Requirements depend on the reason for the change.

3. Correction of Name or Address

Check the correction service and supporting evidence accepted by the issuing authority. Submit only through an official channel.

4. e-Ration Card / Download

Look for the available download or print option on the relevant official portal. Availability and login requirements may vary.

5. Application Status

Use the acknowledgement or application reference number on the official status page, if provided.

6. Ration Shop and Entitlement

Check applicable entitlement, fair-price shop details and distribution information using the relevant state or national service.

7. Complaint or Grievance

Use the department's official grievance channel for card errors, distribution issues or other complaints. Keep your complaint reference number.

Official links

Maharashtra Food Department ↗ National Food Security Portal ↗ PDS Portal ↗

Exact services, eligibility, documents, fees and processing times can change. Verify them on the official portal before applying.

Folder 02 · Madhya Pradesh

Samagra Portal

Samagra Family ID, Member ID aur Madhya Pradesh Samagra social-security services ki guidance.

  • Samagra Family ID information
  • Samagra Member ID search
  • New family / member registration guidance
  • Member details update and correction
  • e-KYC and Aadhaar-related guidance

1. Samagra Family ID

Samagra is a Madhya Pradesh state family and member database. Use the official portal to find available family ID search and family-related services.

2. Samagra Member ID

Use the member search service, if available, and enter only the information requested by the official portal.

3. New Family or Member Registration

Check the current eligibility, registration route and required supporting documents. Do not assume every registration is available online.

4. Member Details Correction

Follow the correction option available for the particular field. Supporting documents and verification may be required.

5. e-KYC / Aadhaar Linkage

Check the current e-KYC process and status options on the official portal. Never share your OTP, password or Aadhaar authentication codes with an unofficial helper.

6. Family Member Add / Remove

Choose the appropriate official service and follow the applicable verification process. A change may require approval by the responsible authority.

7. Status and Assistance

Keep your application or request reference number if one is issued. Use the official help or grievance route for unresolved issues.

Official link

Samagra Official Portal ↗ Madhya Pradesh Government ↗

Samagra is for Madhya Pradesh; it is not Maharashtra's ration card or citizen-service database. Verify current steps and eligibility on the official portal.
