Folder 01 · Food & Public Distribution

Ration Card Services

Maharashtra and Central Government food distribution guidance in one place.

  • New ration card application guidance
  • Add or remove a family member
  • Name, address and details correction
  • e-Ration Card and application status
  • Ration entitlement, FPS shop and grievance

1. New Ration Card

Check eligibility, household category, residence requirements and the application route on the official state portal.

  • Identity and address proof as specified by the department
  • Family member details and required declarations
  • Existing ration card information, if applicable
  • Additional documents may be required

2. Add or Remove a Family Member

Choose the relevant service and keep the existing card and supporting family documents ready. Requirements depend on the reason for the change.

3. Name or Address Correction

Check which correction service is available and which supporting evidence the issuing authority accepts.

4. e-Ration Card / Download

Look for the available download or print option on the official portal. Login requirements may vary.

5. Application Status

Use the acknowledgement or application reference number on the official status page, if provided.

6. Ration Shop and Entitlement

Check applicable entitlement, fair-price shop details and distribution information through the relevant state or national service.

7. Complaint or Grievance

Use the department's grievance channel for card errors, distribution issues or other complaints. Keep your complaint reference.

Official links

Maharashtra Food Department ↗ National Food Security ↗ PDS Portal ↗

Verify current eligibility, documents, fees and processing times on the official portal before applying.

Folder 02 · Madhya Pradesh

Samagra Portal

Samagra Family ID, Member ID and Madhya Pradesh social-security service guidance.

  • Samagra Family ID information
  • Samagra Member ID search
  • New family or member registration guidance
  • Member details update and correction
  • e-KYC and Aadhaar-related guidance

1. Samagra Family ID

Samagra is a Madhya Pradesh state family and member database. Use the official portal for available family ID search services.

2. Samagra Member ID

Use the member search service, if available, and enter only the details requested by the official portal.

3. New Family or Member Registration

Check current eligibility, registration options and required documents. Not every registration may be available online.

4. Member Details Correction

Follow the official correction process. Supporting documents and verification may be required.

5. e-KYC / Aadhaar Linkage

Check the current e-KYC process on the official portal. Never share OTPs, passwords or authentication codes with unofficial helpers.

6. Family Member Add / Remove

Choose the applicable service and follow the verification process. Changes may require approval by the responsible authority.

7. Status and Assistance

Keep your application reference number, if issued. Use the official help or grievance route for unresolved issues.

Official links

Samagra Official Portal ↗ Madhya Pradesh Government ↗

Samagra is for Madhya Pradesh, not Maharashtra's ration-card database. Verify current steps on the official portal.
