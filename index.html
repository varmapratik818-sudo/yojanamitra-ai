📋 Zaroori documents ki checklist

  • Aadhaar card aur bank account details
  • 7/12 utara aur 8A, jahan applicable ho
  • Fasal, kheti aur nuksan ki details
  • Panchnama, beneficiary number ya VK number, agar mila ho
  • Fasal bima policy details, agar fasal insured hai

📝 Kisan ko kya karna chahiye?

  1. Talathi ya Krishi Sahayak se nuksan survey ki sthiti poochhein.
  2. Tahsil office se pata karein ki aapke gaon/taluke ke liye relief order lagu hai ya nahi.
  3. Beneficiary list aur record mein naam check karein.
  4. e-KYC ya document correction ki zaroorat ho to official instructions follow karein.
  5. Bank payment status verify karein aur problem ho to Tahsil office mein complaint karein.

Documents aur process case ke hisaab se badal sakte hain. Kisi agent ko payment na karein bina official confirmation ke.
