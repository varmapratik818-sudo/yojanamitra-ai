Before You Apply

Application checklist

  • Eligibility aur last date verify karein.
  • Documents ki list official instructions se match karein.
  • Name, date of birth, bank details aur mobile number carefully check karein.
  • Application number/receipt ko save karein.
  • Sirf official portal par hi sensitive details submit karein.

Fraud se kaise bachein

“Guaranteed job”, “scholarship pakki” ya application approve karane ke badle personal UPI par paise maangne wale messages par bharosa na karein. OTP, password ya bank PIN kisi ko na batayein. Koi bhi fee ho to uska official portal par mention verify karein.
